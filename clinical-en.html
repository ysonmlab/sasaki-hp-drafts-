Clinical Laboratory

The Department of Laboratory Medicine works closely with the Clinical Laboratory of Institute of Science Tokyo Hospital to provide the clinical testing essential for diagnosing and treating patients. Across a broad range of testing areas — including hematology, biochemistry, immunology, and microbiology — we are committed to delivering highly accurate testing and advancing the field of laboratory medicine.
