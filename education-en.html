EDUCATION & GRADUATE SCHOOL

Join Us!

Join us in pushing forward the frontiers of hematologic oncology, infectious disease, and laboratory medicine. The Department of Laboratory Medicine welcomes students and researchers from diverse backgrounds through several graduate programs at the Institute of Science Tokyo. In a research environment spanning basic science to clinical application, we cultivate the next generation of researchers and clinicians.

For Undergraduate Students

We accept inquiries for senior thesis research, off-campus internships, and lab tours on a rolling basis.
If you are an undergraduate interested in research on hematologic malignancies or laboratory medicine, please feel free to contact us.

Our Educational Philosophy

Our laboratory aims to cultivate independent researchers and clinicians, with education centered on the following three priorities.

  • 01

    Independent Research Skills

    We foster the ability to set your own research themes, form hypotheses, and test them. Weekly meetings and one-on-one mentoring provide careful support in developing your research.

  • 02

    Global Communication Skills

    We actively support presentations at domestic and international conferences and the writing of English-language papers. Our international collaboration with MD Anderson Cancer Center allows students to build a global research network.

  • 03

    Bridging Clinical Practice and Research

    From the perspective of laboratory medicine, we pursue research themes directly connected to patient care, advancing translational research that bridges basic science and clinical practice.

FAQ

QCan I join if I did not study medicine as an undergraduate?
AYes, we welcome you. Our students come from a variety of backgrounds, including science, pharmacy, engineering, and the life sciences. Enthusiasm for research matters most.
QCan I visit the laboratory?
AYes, we accept visits at any time. Please contact us by email first, and we will explain the laboratory atmosphere and our current research themes.
QIs support available for scholarships or fellowships?
AWe actively support applications for JSPS Research Fellowships and various scholarships. Please contact us individually for details.
